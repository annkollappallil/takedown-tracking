Auditing Methodology & Scientific Standards

Eligibility & Denominators

Removal compliance denominators include all formally completed police takedown requests. Procedurally incomplete requests (missing court orders, invalid URLs, unspecified jurisdiction) are quarantined to ensure platforms are evaluated only on actionable, legally complete demands.

No Acknowledgement as Removal

Intermediary confirmation of receipt is audited as acknowledgement speed, never as proof of content removal. Unresolved requests remain marked as still available and penalize the deadline compliance metric.

k-Anonymity (k=10) Protection

To eliminate victim re-identification or targeted correlation attacks, any category or platform cohort containing fewer than 10 records is automatically suppressed from public dashboards and API outputs.

Reproducible Calculations

All medians and 90th percentiles use standardized linear interpolation between closest ranks. Full metric formulas and mathematical proofs are published openly in METHODOLOGY.md.
